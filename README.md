# Earl.e-Bird-Special-Cisco-Learning-
Cisco Learning for CCNA &amp; CCNP

Hello everyone my name is Armond, and I've gotten a lot of requests to teach people "Cisco" which is basic entry to networking.  The Cisco Certified Network Associates (CCNA) is the start of your education and career path in this field. This has become a topic of interest in this Discord Server and I am willing to help teach it for anyone willing to learn.

I have had my CCNA & CCNP in Route & Switch, as well as my CCNA Voice Certifications.  Though they expire after 3 years and if you don't continue education you lose them and have to recertify, like me.  Don't do what I did, if you can help it.  :wink:  Though it still means a lot on a resume to jobs when you apply in the future and will give a broader knowledge of computers and networking systems.
I've started this project using GNS3 & GNS3VM.  These are both free to download and setup on Windows, MacOS, Linux/Unix environments.  They are applications that can simulate Cisco Networking Equipment like Routers & Switches to configure and study for your exams.  They can be downloaded from "https://gns3.com/software/download".  Please get the GNS3 File for your OS, as well as the small link below it saying GNS3VM.  Once downloaded and installed, if you are having any trouble, please visit their documentations page for help, here "https://docs.gns3.com/". If you still have problems, please ask in the appropriate Discord channels here for advice/help and hopefully someone can get back with you as quickly as possible.

I've created a rough topology for now that will cover basic Network+ and CCNA commands and configurations you would see on the actual exams if you decide to take them.  I will expand on it as we learn and improve our skills to push you to the CCNP Level. I will upload a photo of the Topology below in this article plus have it on our Community GitHub repository. Other files will be added there so that you can download them yourself and recreate it in GNS3 to follow along with everyone participating.  I have also put all the starter topology devices, configuration files, on the GitHub Repository.  This will allow you to copy and paste them into devices to get started quicker.  So that way, if you want to follow a long with a course we do live, you'd be able to either type all the commands yourself or upload the files into your devies to get them running.  We will start small, covering the basics of Networking and CCNA Course Materials to help you study for the exams, and/or just get a better grasp of Networking in general to apply to your hacking skills that will help you greatly to manuver around a network easier and make you understand what you are looking at when you log into networking equipment.
Use this information to apply to your own skills/projects within GNS3 & GNS3VM.
Create and share your own projects with us as well.  It would be nice to see other(s) projects and also help troubleshoot them.  Maybe we'll learn something from you that you can contribute to the group as well in the future.
